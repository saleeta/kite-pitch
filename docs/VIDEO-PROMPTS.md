# Kite: 12 self-contained video prompts (6 sections x 2 scenes, ~15 s each)

Each prompt below is complete. Paste one at a time into the video generator. The shared PRODUCT, STYLE and AVOID text is repeated in each so every clip works on its own and the series stays visually consistent.

Tip: generate the first scene, then use its still frame as a reference image for the rest. Tag the final video 'concept footage' for judges.

Note: scene 1A uses a dolly-zoom look. The real Tello has a fixed lens, so for real footage it would be a backward flight with a crop-in; the generated version can show the true effect.

## Section 1: Take off and steer with the ball

### Scene 1A: Mountain dolly zoom with an imaginary rope

Feature shown: Setting a waypoint, pinch control, solo photography made easy

```text
Create a 15-second commercial-style video ad titled 'Kite: Mountain dolly zoom with an imaginary rope'. This is one scene in a series of 12 for a product called Kite.

PRODUCT: Kite is a drone studio you wear. One person wears slim black smart glasses (Meta Ray-Ban Display style) and flies a small white camera drone from the glasses' heads-up view. The view is a 600x600 black overlay, never a phone screen: one glowing ball you pinch and hold to steer the drone (release the ball and the drone stops instantly), a ring, and big buttons Take off, Land, Stop. The user can draw a camera move in the air or set waypoints, and Kite smooths it into a flight arc. A safety governor checks every command and refuses unsafe ones with a short reason. Shots are small reusable files that can be flown again. Kite can orbit or follow a person, holds forward motion with an amber 'something ahead' warning, and works from a wrist band. A small Arduino UNO Q board relays drone control and video so the laptop keeps its internet. After landing, the film is published to the cloud, given a named look (Noir, Spotlight, Steady), trimmed, captioned and shared by a QR code on the lens, with no manual editing.

SCENE: A lone hiker stands on a rocky summit at sunrise, sea of cloud and jagged peaks behind. A small white drone hovers 10 m in front of them. 0-3s: wide drone shot, hiker pinches the air. Insert: glasses overlay shows a glowing ring pinned in the sky with the label 'Waypoint set'. 3-9s: hiker grins, grabs an imaginary rope and pulls hand over hand toward their chest. The drone shot is a perfect dolly zoom (vertigo effect): the hiker stays exactly the same size while the mountains behind swell and stretch dramatically. Speed ramp and a deep 'whoomp' sound. 9-13s: hiker mock-staggers backward, laughing, as the background settles, then gives a thumbs-up. Cut to the drone view of the whole summit.

ON-SCREEN CAPTION (short, bold, lower third, appears around second 11): 'Waypoint set. Reel it in.'

Voiceover: 'Alone on a mountain? Set a waypoint, pull the shot in, no crew needed.'

STYLE: Premium high-energy TV commercial, like a flagship tech product launch spot. Fast, readable cuts, whip pans, speed ramps, kinetic overlay graphics, saturated golden-hour colour, shallow depth of field, anamorphic lens flares, crisp 4K look. Alternate between (a) cinematic drone-camera shots, (b) over-the-shoulder shots of the person, and (c) quick inserts of the glasses' heads-up overlay. Joyful, playful, confident tone. Audio: punchy upbeat electronic music with a strong beat, whooshes on cuts, a soft chime when the overlay confirms something, natural drone propeller hum, and a warm energetic voiceover. 16:9, 15 seconds. Lighting and colours consistent with the other clips in the series: warm gold, deep teal shadows, lime-green accent on the UI ring and ball.

END CARD (last 1.5 s): the word KITE with the line 'Direct the shot from where you see it.' on a dark background with the lime ring.

AVOID: real brand logos or trademarks, readable fine text other than the quoted overlay captions, phone screens as the controller, a handheld remote controller, distorted hands, extra fingers, warped drone, melting faces, watermarks, shaky unwatchable footage.
```

### Scene 6A: Landed, published, edited in one breath

Feature shown: Publish, named looks, trim, caption, no manual editing

```text
Create a 15-second commercial-style video ad titled 'Kite: Landed, published, edited in one breath'. This is one scene in a series of 12 for a product called Kite.

PRODUCT: Kite is a drone studio you wear. One person wears slim black smart glasses (Meta Ray-Ban Display style) and flies a small white camera drone from the glasses' heads-up view. The view is a 600x600 black overlay, never a phone screen: one glowing ball you pinch and hold to steer the drone (release the ball and the drone stops instantly), a ring, and big buttons Take off, Land, Stop. The user can draw a camera move in the air or set waypoints, and Kite smooths it into a flight arc. A safety governor checks every command and refuses unsafe ones with a short reason. Shots are small reusable files that can be flown again. Kite can orbit or follow a person, holds forward motion with an amber 'something ahead' warning, and works from a wrist band. A small Arduino UNO Q board relays drone control and video so the laptop keeps its internet. After landing, the film is published to the cloud, given a named look (Noir, Spotlight, Steady), trimmed, captioned and shared by a QR code on the lens, with no manual editing.

SCENE: A creator lands the drone on her open palm. She presses 'Publish' on the lens; a progress ring fills with 'Uploading 2 of 3'. The footage appears three times side by side, labelled Noir, Spotlight and Steady, swiping across the overlay. She picks Noir, drags a trim handle, speaks a caption, and the finished film plays on a floating screen. Show no laptop, no timeline, no editing software anywhere. Quick, snappy interface animation.

ON-SCREEN CAPTION (short, bold, lower third, appears around second 11): 'Publish. Pick a look. Trim. Caption. Done.'

Voiceover: 'After landing: publish, pick a look, trim, caption. Your film is done, no editing software.'

STYLE: Premium high-energy TV commercial, like a flagship tech product launch spot. Fast, readable cuts, whip pans, speed ramps, kinetic overlay graphics, saturated golden-hour colour, shallow depth of field, anamorphic lens flares, crisp 4K look. Alternate between (a) cinematic drone-camera shots, (b) over-the-shoulder shots of the person, and (c) quick inserts of the glasses' heads-up overlay. Joyful, playful, confident tone. Audio: punchy upbeat electronic music with a strong beat, whooshes on cuts, a soft chime when the overlay confirms something, natural drone propeller hum, and a warm energetic voiceover. 16:9, 15 seconds. Lighting and colours consistent with the other clips in the series: warm gold, deep teal shadows, lime-green accent on the UI ring and ball.

END CARD (last 1.5 s): the word KITE with the line 'Direct the shot from where you see it.' on a dark background with the lime ring.

AVOID: real brand logos or trademarks, readable fine text other than the quoted overlay captions, phone screens as the controller, a handheld remote controller, distorted hands, extra fingers, warped drone, melting faces, watermarks, shaky unwatchable footage.
```
