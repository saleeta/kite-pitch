WOW FACTORS

Three moments that make people stop

Pull the shot in

Set a waypoint on a summit, pull an imaginary rope, and only the background grows. A solo dolly zoom.

The ball is the controller

Pinch and hold to fly. Let go and the drone stops. No remote, no looking down.

Land to link

Publish, pick a look, scan a QR code on your lens. The film is on a phone, with no editing.

These are the three beats to hit in the video and at the table. Each one is something a judge can repeat to someone else in one sentence.
